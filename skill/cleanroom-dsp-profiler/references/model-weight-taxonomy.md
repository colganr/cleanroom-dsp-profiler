# Model Weight & Tensor Taxonomy — Diagnostic Troubleshooting Table

When a strictly held-out verification test shows low Waveform Correlation (`Corr %`), elevated Error-to-Signal Ratio (`ESR dB`), or Spectral Error (`Spec Err dB > 0.5 dB`), use this lookup table to pinpoint which tensor or physical stage is responsible:

| # | Weight / Tensor | Location | Physical Behavior Modeled | Diagnostic Symptom If Wrong or Missing | Required Measurement & Fix |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **`BASE_PRE_IR`** ($H_{\text{pre}}$) | `amp_weights.rs` (`[f32; 1024]`) | Input coupling cap, V1 plate load, nominal tone stack, interstage RC **before** clipping. | High frequencies rolled off before the clipper if $H_{\text{tot}}$ wasn't deconvolved (`Corr < 89%` on high-gain). | Capture settled bipolar `±1e-4` IR and deconvolve $H_{\text{post}}$ in FFT domain. |
| 2 | **`BASE_POST_IR`** ($H_{\text{post}}$) | `amp_weights.rs` (`[f32; 64]`) | Output transformer roll-off, negative feedback damping, post-clipping de-fizz (`-20 to -33 dB > 3 kHz`). | Fizzy, harsh highs (`Spec Err > 2.5 dB`) and phase-skewed clipping harmonics (`3f/5f/7f`). | Measure via hard-saturated quadrature lock-in sine sweeps (`measure_post_ir_hard_sat`). |
| 3 | **`PRE_SWITCH_COMBOS_DB`** | `[[f32; 64]; 2^K]` | Non-additive loading across all $2^K$ passive front-end switches (`Bright`, `Mid`, `Deep`, `EQ`). | Engaging `>= 2` switches overshoots gain by **`+10 to +12 dB`** (`ESR > +6 dB`) if additive deltas were used. | Capture all $2^K$ bitmask states directly via `capture_switch_combo_tensor`. |
| 4 | **`TONESTACK_3D_DB`** | `[[[[f32; 64]; 5]; 5]; 5]` | Coupled passive `Bass × Middle × Treble` network (indexed by cap-altering switches like `Mid Boost`). | `> 2 dB` midrange error when tone knobs move away from `0.5` with `Mid Boost` active. | Capture $5 \times 5 \times 5$ grid per cap-switch state; subtract center `[2][2][2]` at runtime. |
| 5 | **`PRE_GAIN_BODE_21_DB`** | `[[f32; 64]; 21]` | Pre-clipping gain pot taper + **bright-cap treble bleed** across wiper travel. | Treble balance drifts whenever `Gain != 0.5`. Must route strictly to **`H_pre`**. | Sweep `21` steps (`0.01..1.00`) with `±1e-4` bipolar impulses. |
| 6 | **`POST_EQ_BODE_21_DB`** | `[[f32; 64]; 21]` | `Master` volume taper, `Presence` negative-feedback damping, power-tube switches (`Half Power`). | Turning `Master` alters preamp saturation instead of scaling output linearly. Must route to **`H_post`**. | Sweep `21` steps (`0.01..1.00`) with `±1e-4` impulses; fold into 64-tap `active_post_ir`. |
| 7 | **Bounded Triode Bloom (`sat_0`)** | `AmpCoreConfig` (`amp_core.rs`) | Cold-biased 12AX7 mid-level `+1.5 dB` gain expansion before rail saturation. | Clean / edge-of-breakup ESR stuck at `-9 to -11 dB` (plain `tanh` compresses too early); unbounded bloom explodes at high gain. | Tune `cp0/cn0` & `bloom_alpha` while keeping $\text{sat}_0'(0) = 1.0$. |
| 8 | **Clean-Room KONE Reservoir** | `reservoir.rs` / `reservoir.py` | Multi-scale recurrent memory (`96 kHz` 2× oversampled, `16` amp / `8` pedal states) + sag/bias modulation. | Dynamic pick-attack compression and note decay feel flat (`Corr` plateaus around `97–98%`). | Ensure Python & Rust reservoirs match bit-for-bit (`test_python_rust_parity.py`). |
| 9 | **`AMP_WEIGHTS` (`180`-ch × brackets)** | `amp_weights.rs` | 3-pole (`180/720/2400 Hz`) + coupled `V1→V2` + `96 kHz` KONE + `35/383 Hz` sag recovery. | Held-out scale collapse or overfit fizz if trained without scale-penalized ridge or on silent audio. | Train on active `4.0–8.0s` segment through $H_{\text{post}}$ with `solve_scale_penalized_ridge`. |
| 10 | **`OD_PRE_IR_21` & `OD_POST_IR_21`** | `pedal_weights.rs` (`[f32; 256]`) | Pedal pre-clipping `Drive` mid-hump vs. post-clipping `Tone` low-pass roll-off. | Diode harmonics above `1.5 kHz` sound overly bright (`Spec Err > 3 dB`) if `POST_IR` at `Tone=0.5` is left flat. | Deconvolve `POST_IR` from `PRE_IR`; refine `POST_IR_21` around `Drive=0.5`. |
| 11 | **`OD_WEIGHTS` (`126`-ch × `9` brackets)** | `pedal_weights.rs` | 3-pole frequency-selective op-amp/diode feedback loop across `[0.00..1.00]` Drive. | Mis-matched clipping symmetry or blowup when stacked after a hot boost pedal. | Set `subtract_dry` to match circuit topology; include hot `0.40–0.70` peaks in calibration stimulus. |
| 12 | **`CAB_GRID_SCALES` + `.bin`** | `cab_grid.rs` (`i16 + f32`) | Spatial speaker/mic acoustic transfer function (2D `Pos × Dist` or 3D `Axis × Dist × Angle`). | Overall tone / brightness off or wrong volume when blending `>= 2` mics. | Verify `level_match` (`1 / N_active`), mic index decoding, and unity cabinet level calibration. |
| 13 | **`NAM v0.5.4` WaveNet Weights** | `.nam` JSON (`nam_export.py`) | Distilled 10-layer causal dilated WaveNet (`dilations=[1..512]`) snapshot for hardware players. | Loader rejects file (weight count mismatch) or metallic artifacts (reverb/gate left active). | Verify with `expected_wavenet_weight_count()` + `forward_pass_nam_v054()`; auto-bypass time-based FX. |
